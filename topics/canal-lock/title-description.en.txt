Description
Canals at different heights are connected by locks.
Change the water level in the chamber, and the floating boat rises or falls with it.
Why the front gate has to wait, and how water does all the lifting, shown in simple 3D animation.

Final English narration
This boat climbs to the next level while still floating. Unbelievable, right?
So where's the machine that lifts it?
The canal ahead is high. The boat's side is low.
First, the chamber matches the low side, the boat moves in, and the gate behind it closes.
Now open the channel from the high side, and water flows in, lifting the boat with it.
The water enters through a small channel. The floor never moves; only the boat floats up.
But the front gate can't open yet.
While the levels differ, water would rush through hard.
Once the levels match, the channel closes and the front gate opens to let the boat out.
Going down, you just let water out to the low side.
No moving floor. It's an elevator that moves boats with water.
That's a canal lock.
